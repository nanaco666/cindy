# Code of Conduct

We want the Cindy community to be safe, respectful, and inclusive. When
participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, or other
community activities, please:

- respect people with different backgrounds, experience, and opinions;
- use clear, kind, and problem-focused language;
- accept constructive feedback and focus on the code, documentation, or
  behavior;
- do not harass, discriminate, threaten, expose private information, or make
  personal attacks;
- do not publish credentials, personal data, unpatched vulnerabilities, or
  other sensitive information.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict access to community resources, and
take other reasonable actions when this code is violated. For security
vulnerabilities, follow [`SECURITY.en.md`](SECURITY.en.md) and do not disclose
exploitable details publicly. For other conduct concerns, contact the
maintainers privately through GitHub or `feedback@cindy.app`.
